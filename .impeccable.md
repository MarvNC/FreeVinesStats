## Design Context

### Users
Amazon Vine power users and frequent deal hunters who check drop patterns obsessively throughout the day. They understand Vine jargon (0 ETV, AFA, AI) and want dense, actionable data at a glance. Often checking on mobile between drops. Emotional state: anticipatory, slightly anxious about missing good items, but also enjoying the hunt.

### Brand Personality
- **Voice:** Insider-friendly, playful, scrappy
- **Tone:** Like a tool built by a fellow Vine user, not a corporation
- **3 words:** Playful, deal-hunting, insider
- **Emotional goals:** Delight frequent users, make data feel fun rather than clinical

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Visual tone:** Playful data density — rich with information but never sterile
- **References:** None provided — open to creative direction
- **Anti-references:** Corporate dashboards, utilitarian tools, overly minimal interfaces
- **Theme:** Both light and dark modes, toggleable. Light should feel bright and energetic (morning browsing), dark should feel cozy and focused (late-night drop hunting)

### Design Principles
1. **Insider energy:** The interface should feel like it was built by someone who actually uses Vine, with personality and warmth
2. **Playful density:** Information-rich for power users, but with delightful details that reward frequent visits
3. **Blue-forward but distinctive:** Blue is the anchor color, but the palette should feel unique and memorable
4. **Mode intentionality:** Light mode = bright, caffeinated, ready-to-hunt. Dark mode = focused, cozy, late-night sessions
5. **Anti-corporate:** Avoid sterile metrics, generic card grids, and templated dashboard patterns
